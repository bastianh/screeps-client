Energy visualisation for structures in the room view and selection panel:

- **Spawns** now show their energy in the selection details panel (matching extensions).
- **Links** render an energy-proportional diamond core in the room view that scales with stored energy, and show Energy (plus Cooldown) in the details panel.
- **Sources** render a fixed dark base with a golden core that shrinks as they are mined, revealing a dark ring; when exhausted the centre is black and only the outer ring pulses to signal regeneration.
